HISTORICAL PRECEDENT // NSSDCA 1967-035A

In November 1969, Apollo 12 (`1969-099C`) landed 160 meters from robotic lander Surveyor 3 (`1967-035A`). Astronauts Pete Conrad and Alan Bean retrieved Surveyor 3’s television camera; laboratory analysis revealed severe surface pitting and cracking caused by high-velocity lunar regolith accelerated by Apollo 12’s descent engine plume.
